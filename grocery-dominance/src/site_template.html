    How a hex is won

    The US is cut into H3 resolution-4 hexes (about 680 sq mi each). A hex goes to whoever leads across the hex plus its six neighbours. Only hexes with at least one grocery store are drawn.

    The measures

    Store names and parents come from Overture Maps places. Floor space is each store's building footprint. Estimated sales spread each chain's latest US grocery revenue across its stores by floor space; independents get $400 per sq ft.

    Independents

    About 62% of store records are unbranded independents. On the parent maps each competes as its own owner, so tan marks places where no chain beats a single local store. The "Independents pooled" view treats them as one company.
